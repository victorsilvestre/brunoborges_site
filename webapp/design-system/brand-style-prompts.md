# Brand Style Prompts

## Prompt for Visual Design

Create a visual design inspired by the analyzed brand style.

Key characteristics:
- Modern dark mode with very high contrast and medium-high visual density, simulating a finance tech platform or a pro trader's environment.
- Headlines should use clean, geometric, and very bold sans-serif fonts in All-Caps, occasionally juxtaposed with an elegant, flowing handwriting/script font for key specific emotional words (like signatures).
- The color palette is strictly on an absolute black or very dark grey background, using bright "bull-market green" and "bear-market red" as pure accent and highlight colors, and pure white for main text readability.
- Make use of photography showcasing real professional trading screens and candlestick charts seamlessly blended into dark backgrounds with a soft blur or vignette, serving as a moody backdrop.
- Include dynamic diagonal cuts or banners, circular windows with neon frames revealing charts, up-arrow accents, and thick quotes or bullet points.

## Prompt for Image Generation

Generate an image in the style of the analyzed brand.

Style characteristics:
- Dark, intense, real-world technological environment representing a serious day trader's setup.
- Accents of pure green and red lighting against a very dark/black room canvas.
- Focus heavily on out-of-focus computer monitors displaying candlestick charts, leaving vast areas of dark negative space purposely free for subsequent typography insertion.
- The treatment should be cinematic, moody, with crushed blacks and high contrast on the glowing screen areas.

## Prompt for Social Media Content

Create a social media post in the style of the analyzed brand.

Tone of voice:
- Assertive, direct, action-oriented, and highly motivational in a strict way (challenging the reader, emphasizing discipline, effort, and high rewards).

Visual style:
- High contrast typography layout over a dark/black canvas. Use a huge, thick white headline with one or two key words handwritten in cursive green or red. Add minimal, sharp geometric shapes like diagonal ribbons for specific tags.

---

## Prompt for Landing Page

Create a landing page concept following this brand style.

Include:
- A very dark, impactful hero section with a blurred background showing trading screens. The headline must jump out in huge white and red, strong sans caps mixed with a script-font subtext overlay.
- Large, bold sans-serif headlines paired with highly readable and neat lower-weight sans-serif body text contexts.
- Strict discipline to a dark mode palette (black/#111) with vibrant green (#23A455 or similar) for primary Call-to-Action buttons and bright red for urgency/alerts.
- High-contrast section dividers using diagonal lines, and large metric cards mimicking trading dashboards, avoiding overwhelming full-screen brightness at all times.
